Stage zero · before you spend a dollar

Ads don't build the trust. Your page does.

Nobody opens Instagram saying "I hope I see an ad today." Your ad is a disruption to their regularly scheduled programming. When one actually lands — when someone thinks holy shit, this is exactly what I'm looking for — the very next thing they do is go and check your page to see whether you're a real person. If there's nothing there with your face on it, you paid for that click twice and got nothing.

The part most people miss

Skool is already running ads for you. Cold ones.

Growth Boost means Skool pays to run ads promoting your community. Every single person who sees one is cold traffic — they didn't come from your content, they've never heard of you, and they're deciding in about a second and a half.

Which means your offer has to have product-market fit with an audience that knows nothing. Who it is, what it is, who it's for, and how it helps — clear, and clear fast.

So Stages 01 and 02 aren't really about ads. They're you doing your homework on the two assets Skool is about to put in front of strangers, on Skool's budget, whether you tested them or not.

You're not spending money to get members here. You're spending a little to find out what to hand Skool.
