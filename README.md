# The 4 Stages of Ads to Your Skool

An interactive guide to the four Meta ad campaigns I run, in order, to validate an
offer with cold traffic and turn it into paid members of a Skool community.

**Read it here → https://aefieldi.github.io/4-stages-of-ads/**

- **Stage 01** — test the group cover card before Growth Boost spends on it
- **Stage 02** — lock the banner, test the About page copy
- **Stage 03** — engagement and traffic campaigns at $5 a day
- **Stage 04** — the sales campaign, at 2–3× your front-end offer per day

Includes a daily-budget calculator and a 9:16 screenshot mode for short-form.
